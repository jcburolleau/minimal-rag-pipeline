# Amendment 1 to Rhine Silicon supply agreement

This document is part of a **fictional** knowledge base for Northline Sensors SA. It is not a real company, product, or contract.

## Effect

Amendment **AMD-2025-01** amends agreement **C-2024-RS**.
Signed 2025-04-18. Effective **2025-04-20**.

When this amendment and the original agreement disagree, prefer this document.
It is newer.

## Summary

Changes the standard lead time for part RS-HUM-7 from 45 days to 70 days because of a process move at the Dresden fab. Piece price remains EUR 18.40. All other terms of C-2024-RS stay in force.

## Changed clauses

- lead_time_days: 45 → 70
- unit_price_eur: 18.4 → 18.4

## Operational impact

Finished NL-SP-90 probes inherit the 70-day lead time. Northline did not raise the CHF 890 list price of the probe. Safety stock of RS-HUM-7 was increased from four weeks to eight weeks.
