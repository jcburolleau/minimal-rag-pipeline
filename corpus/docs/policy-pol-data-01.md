# Customer telemetry retention

This document is part of a **fictional** knowledge base for Northline Sensors SA. It is not a real company, product, or contract.

## Summary

Policy **POL-DATA-01**, owner Anja Vuković. Dated 2025-01-06.
How long Northline keeps customer sensor frames.

## Policy text

Northline gateways may forward frames to the customer's own broker. When
Northline hosts the broker, frames are kept for 24 months, then deleted.

Northline does not sell telemetry. Support staff may inspect the last
14 days of frames when a ticket is open.
