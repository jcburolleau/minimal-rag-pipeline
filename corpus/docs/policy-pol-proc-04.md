# Procurement policy

This document is part of a **fictional** knowledge base for Northline Sensors SA. It is not a real company, product, or contract.

## Summary

Policy **POL-PROC-04**, owner Martin Keller. Dated 2025-02-01.
How Northline buys parts and services.

## Policy text

Single-source parts require a written dual-source plan within twelve months
of first production use. Baltic Piezo transducers and Rhine Silicon moisture
ICs are currently single-source and are listed on the dual-source register.

Purchase orders over CHF 25 000 need CFO countersignature. Orders over
CHF 80 000 need CEO countersignature.

Lead times published on product sheets must match the live supplier lead
time, including amendments. The NL-SP-90 sheet is the reference example:
it tracks AMD-2025-01, not the original C-2024-RS clause.
