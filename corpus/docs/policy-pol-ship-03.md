# Shipping and Incoterms

This document is part of a **fictional** knowledge base for Northline Sensors SA. It is not a real company, product, or contract.

## Summary

Policy **POL-SHIP-03**, owner Jonas Meier. Dated 2024-07-15.
Default shipping terms for finished goods.

## Policy text

Default sale term is DAP customer site in Switzerland, DAP Basel for EU
customers, and FCA Lausanne for all other destinations.

Lithium batteries ship as UN3481. Alpine Cells packs above 100 Wh go as
cargo-aircraft only.

Calibration kits with liquid buffers cannot travel in the same carton as
lithium packs.
