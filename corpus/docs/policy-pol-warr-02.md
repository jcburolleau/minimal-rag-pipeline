# Warranty and field service

This document is part of a **fictional** knowledge base for Northline Sensors SA. It is not a real company, product, or contract.

## Summary

Policy **POL-WARR-02**, owner Camille Dufour. Dated 2024-10-01.
Cover for instruments sold after 1 October 2024.

## Policy text

Hardware warranty is three years from ship date, parts and labour, provided
field recalibration uses NL-CAL-KIT or a Northline service day.

Water-quality sondes (NL-WQ-3) require annual calibration. Missed
calibration suspends warranty on the optical window only.

Glacier stakes (NL-ICE-2) are warranted against freeze damage. They are not
warranted against burial, avalanche, or crevasse loss.
